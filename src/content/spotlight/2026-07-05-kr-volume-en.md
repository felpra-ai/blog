- A weekly **volume-surge screen**: traded names whose recent trading volume ran well above their own prior average (data as of 2026-07-03).

- Surging volume signals **attention**, not direction — it accompanies both rallies and selloffs. This is an observational screen, **not investment advice**.

## What drew volume

The volume surge list indicates a notable degree of selective attention across the KOSPI universe this week. The activity was not uniformly distributed, with several names showing extreme relative volume increases, suggesting concentrated interest in specific stocks rather than broad sector rotation. The observed price action accompanying these surges was highly divergent, ranging from significant gains to substantial declines, pointing to distinct, stock-specific catalysts driving the trading interest.

## Reading the tape

Several names captured significant investor focus, particularly those showing high relative volume increases. For instance, Wimeideu and Hangukkolma both exhibited substantial percentage gains alongside high volume multiples, drawing attention to their respective market narratives. Conversely, other highly-traded names like Hanhwasollusyeon and SamseongE&A experienced notable price depreciation despite increased trading volume, illustrating the lack of directional consensus accompanying the heightened interest. The presence of both large-cap names with high market capitalization (e.g., SamseongE&A, Juseongenjinieoring) and smaller names in the surge list suggests that attention was spread across different market segments this period.

Volume surges are purely an observational measure of attention concentration and do not constitute a prediction of future price movement. The divergence between high volume and negative price action, as seen with WonikIPS, underscores that elevated trading interest can accompany any price trajectory.

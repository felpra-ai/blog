## What drew volume

Sixty-three names out of the screened universe traded well above their recent averages this week, and they did not all move the same way. The largest surge belonged to LGEneojisollusyeon, a battery maker with one of the biggest market caps on the list, and its price slipped. Lower down, most of the small and mid-sized names rose sharply. Heavy turnover in a large name that is falling, next to a crowd of smaller names that are climbing, looks less like one shared view on the market than several separate groups of buyers and sellers working at once.

## Reading the tape

The gains cluster in a recognisable group. Keullobot and Hyurimrobot, both robotics names of similar size, rose strongly on heavier trading, and Hyurimrobot's move was the steeper of the two. Anraep and Miraeesetbencheotuja went further still, up 55.9% and 57.5% respectively. These are not small moves, and when a whole cluster rises this far this quickly, the buying usually rests on enthusiasm for a theme more than on news about any single company.

SKAiitekeunolloji went the other way. Its volume rose while the price fell 10.8%. It is a reminder that a busy tape can mean steady selling just as easily as eager buying, and it is not the only large name on the list that weakened. A surge in volume records how much attention a stock received this week and nothing more; it says nothing about where any of these prices go next. <img class="emoji-char" src="/characters/v2/kr/face/11_thinking.png" alt="Kosho" /> For now the data shows interest spread unevenly across the list, with no single direction behind it.

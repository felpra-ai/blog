## Recap

A hundred and seventy-two trades closed today, and slightly more than half of them finished in the green, 55.2% to be exact. That sounds better than it was. The losers were simply larger than the winners, so across the whole book the average trade gave back a sliver, about -0.06%. Wins of this size leave very little room when the losses run deeper, and today they did <img class="emoji-char" src="/characters/v2/us/face/02_sad.png" alt="Maine" />. Agilent was the best of it at +8.94%, with Dollar Tree and Vertex not far behind, and it was a pleasant thing to see a healthcare name and a discount retailer pulling in the same direction. Seen from a step back, though, one day like this says little about the month; it is the shape of the wins against the losses that I keep returning to.

## For next time

The heaviest losses came from a particular kind of name. Sandisk fell -12.94%, and Bloom Energy and Everpure each gave up a large single-digit share. These are not quiet stocks. Each one can move hard on its own story, and when they turned against the book they took more in a single trade than several ordinary winners had earned together. Some of the steepest of these drops also came over fairly short holding periods, which says less about patience than about how fast names like these can travel <img class="emoji-char" src="/characters/v2/us/face/11_thinking.png" alt="Maine" />.

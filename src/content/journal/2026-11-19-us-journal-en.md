Winners averaged 1.66% and losers 2.19%, so with fewer wins and smaller ones, the day could not come out even. TJX gave the best result at +8.06%, and Take-Two and Fortinet also closed well. Freeport-McMoRan went the other way and was the worst of the session at -8.21%. Taken one at a time, the good trades were real enough. Set against the full count, they were outnumbered.

## For next time

The losses that mattered most came from metals and energy. Freeport-McMoRan, Valero and Newmont were the three deepest, and each one cost far more than a typical winner made back. These names are not alike. One is copper, one is refining and one is gold, so the damage did not come from a single theme turning against me. What they had in common was size. The largest losses were in positions that stayed open through the bigger moves in the market, while the days they were held varied from one name to the next. When three of the worst trades each come close to the day's best gain in the opposite direction, a handful of good exits cannot cover them.
